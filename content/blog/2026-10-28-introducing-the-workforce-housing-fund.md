The North Fork Housing Alliance is proud to introduce the **Workforce Housing Fund**, a dedicated fund for the preservation and development of affordable housing units serving support personnel and other essential members of the local workforce.

The Fund is specifically designed to address the housing needs of families and individuals whose household income falls between **80% and 120% of the Area Median Income (AMI)**. That "middle-income" or "workforce" bracket is too often underserved by traditional affordable housing programs.

## Why now

Communities run on people and the services and wellbeing they provide. First responders, healthcare workers, teachers, shopkeepers, farmers, baymen and oyster growers are crucial to community vitality, sustainability and quality of life. Many of them are being priced out of the places they serve.

> Every industry, our environment, ecosystem and economy is impacted by housing.

## Building on a long record

Since 1988, NFHA has been a steady and trusted presence on the North Fork, preserving and building affordable housing, administering rental and buying assistance, and standing beside the community's most vulnerable residents. That record, from Housing Choice Voucher administration to senior housing to the Cottages at Mattituck, is the foundation the Fund builds on.

The Fund will be capitalized through both private and public sources. Donations, grants, corporate sponsorships and community fundraising all make a difference. [Support the Fund](/donate) or [get in touch](/work-with-us) about partnering with us.

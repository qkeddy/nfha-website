For nearly four decades, the Greenport-based North Fork Housing Alliance has helped low and moderate income residents find, keep and afford a place to call home.

## What we do every day

- **Operate and maintain rental housing** for low income North Fork residents.
- **Manage the Section 8 rental subsidy program**, making it possible for low and moderate income families to afford living in privately owned rentals.
- **Rehabilitate private properties** for eligible owners.
- **Provide housing counseling** to new homebuyers so local residents can remain in the community in quality housing.

## Our mission

Develop and maintain affordable housing for local families, provide support services for tenants in need, and partner with Federal, State and local agencies to provide and maintain local housing opportunities.

## Our values

**Equity:** promoting fairness and reducing barriers to housing. **Respect:** treating all people fairly and honestly.

That long record of service is the foundation for everything we do next, including the new [Workforce Housing Fund](/the-fund).

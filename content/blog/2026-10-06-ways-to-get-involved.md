It takes a village to build a strong, inclusive, resilient and sustainable community. Here are five ways to be a change maker on the North Fork.

## 1. Give to the Workforce Housing Fund

Every gift helps preserve and develop housing for the people our community depends on. [Make a donation](/donate).

## 2. Become a sponsor

Local businesses and corporate partners can support the Fund through sponsorships, and be recognized alongside our strategic partners.

## 3. Partner with us

We collaborate with organizations, municipalities, developers, builders, contractors and property owners to create housing solutions that benefit everyone. If you have an idea that makes sense and will make an impact, [we want to hear it](/work-with-us).

## 4. Spread the word

Share our story with friends, neighbors and employers. Follow us on social media and forward our updates to someone who needs them.

## 5. Stay informed

Sign up for our newsletter for news on Community and Attainable Housing initiatives. We will never share your information.

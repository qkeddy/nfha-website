The Workforce Housing Fund operates through a multi-pronged development model designed to expand the supply of housing affordable to middle-income households earning 80% to 120% of AMI.

## 1. Purchase existing homes and add an ADU

Where zoning allows within Southold Town, the Fund will purchase existing homes and add an accessory dwelling unit (ADU), creating two distinct rental units from a single parcel. Both the primary home and the new ADU become rental housing.

This approach effectively **doubles the number of households served per property** while making efficient use of existing residential land and infrastructure.

## 2. Multifamily rentals on one parcel

The Fund will pursue acquisitions and partnerships that allow for multiple rentals on a single parcel of land.

## 3. Permanently affordable home ownership

The Fund will also pursue acquisitions and partnerships that result in individually owned, income-restricted homes. Strict caps on resale prices, equity increases and property alterations keep the homes permanently affordable for future moderate-income buyers, similar to the Cottages at Mattituck.

## Intended outcomes

Together these approaches aim to increase the local supply of attainable rental housing, retain the workers who support our community, make productive use of residential property, and provide stable, quality housing to families and individuals who are being priced out of the area.
